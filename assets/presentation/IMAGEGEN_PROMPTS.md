# OquMap poster campaign — ImageGen prompt record

All ten slides were generated as finished 16:9 campaign posters. The common direction was:

> Premium Apple/Stripe-level editorial product campaign; porcelain ivory, graphite, ultramarine/cobalt and restrained ochre; tactile paper, glass and architectural objects; one metaphor and one thesis per frame; bold modern grotesk similar to Noto Sans; exact Russian typography embedded in the raster; no UI cards, dashboards, logos, watermarks, people, generic AI glow or extra text.

The built-in ImageGen tool produced a 3:2 master for each prompt. `prepare_poster_preview.mjs` makes a north-aligned 1920×1080 crop so the embedded typography remains intact.
