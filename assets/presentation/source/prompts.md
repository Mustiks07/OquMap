# Presentation image prompts

## Decision gap

Create a premium editorial still life for an education analytics pitch deck, viewed from above on warm porcelain paper. Arrange fictional assessment sheets, a sparse answer matrix, a curriculum page, and a timetable as disconnected evidence islands. Link some items with thin graphite threads but leave a visible unresolved gap in the middle. Add one restrained ultramarine marker and one small ochre question mark. Civic-humanist art direction, tactile paper, precise museum lighting, no people, no readable text, no logos, no glossy AI-dashboard aesthetic. Wide 16:9 composition with generous dark negative space on the left for a Russian headline. Transparent background where possible.

## Closing atlas horizon

Create a cinematic 16:9 closing image for a premium education analytics pitch deck. A landscape made from layered warm-white curriculum pages and an open paper atlas stretches toward a bright architectural horizon. A thin ultramarine route with seven evidence stations crosses the pages, passes one tiny ochre uncertainty marker and a transparent bridge, then continues into the light. Calm civic-humanist design, tactile paper relief, restrained graphite and porcelain palette, natural museum lighting, no people, no text, no logos, no interface cards. Leave broad quiet negative space on the left for a closing statement.
