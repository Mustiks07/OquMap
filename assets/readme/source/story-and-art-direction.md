# OquMap README story

## Project story

- **Audience:** school methodologists, curriculum leaders, education-data teams, and hackathon judges.
- **One-sentence value:** OquMap turns assessment results into a traceable, reviewable decision about what to teach, which material to test, and how to allocate limited hours.
- **Primary proof:** the Evidence Atlas opens every step from the weakest topic to the calculation, answer rows, curriculum objective, textbook page, hour plan, and recorded decision.
- **First successful action:** `docker compose up --build` → **Открыть демо** → **Демо за 3 минуты**.
- **Visual theme:** Civic Humanist / Evidence Atlas — warm editorial material, measured spacing, blue evidence, ochre uncertainty.

## Project-native hero facts

- **Project category:** evidence-linked curriculum intelligence / EdTech decision support.
- **Main user:** a methodologist responsible for curriculum quality and teaching-time allocation.
- **Main action:** move from a weak learning signal to a defensible, measurable intervention.
- **Best proof:** the real seven-node evidence route and its source inspector.
- **Native visual material:** evidence routes, source pages, heatmaps, raw-versus-standardised comparison, and constrained hour plans.

## Frozen visual system

- **Palette:** porcelain `#F7F6F2`; raised surface `#FCFBF8`; graphite `#17181A`; ultramarine `#3157D5`; uncertainty ochre `#80500F`; muted text `#686C73`.
- **Typography:** system sans-serif with strong editorial scale; monospace only for analysis IDs, versions, and measured metadata.
- **Shape:** restrained `8–20px` radius, `1px` graphite rules at low opacity, `8px` spacing grid.
- **Motif:** one continuous seven-station evidence path that crosses a physical curriculum atlas and leaves an unproven decision visibly open.
- **Composition:** calm, editorial, technical; no gradients, glassmorphism, generic AI brains, or decorative dashboard cards.

## Hybrid subject communication job

The generated transparent paper sculpture makes the abstract product mechanism tangible: evidence accumulates across seven curriculum layers, uncertainty remains visible, and the final decision stays open until a human accepts it. It does not depict the product UI and never replaces the real screenshots immediately below the hero.
